GAME  INFO

About Game

PUBG is a battle royale game where 100 players land on an island and fight to survive. The last player or team wins the match.

Gameplay

Players parachute onto the map, collect weapons, stay inside the safe zone and eliminate enemies to survive.

Game Modes

Play solo, Duo or Squad mode with Friends. Each mode offers unique strategy and teamwork.

Maps

Explore maps like Erangel, Miramar, Sanhok and Vikendi with different terrains and gameplay styles.

Weapons

Use powerful weapons like M416, AKM, sniper rifles and grenades to eliminate your enemies.

Features

Realistic graphics, intense survive gameplay and strategic battles make PUBG exciting.
